About Locus Copilot

Commercial location intelligence for Chennai. Locus Copilot combines a 4,345 micro-locality database, multi-criteria spatial scoring, and live Google Places ground truth data to help retailers make informed site decisions.

The Problem We Address

Opening a commercial storefront often depends on broker anecdotes, high-level demographic aggregates, or guesswork. A retail space in an oversaturated street corner or transit-isolated pocket can lead to costly operational losses. Locus Copilot standardizes site evaluation by evaluating micro-corridors using a transparent, multi-factor scoring model.

Core Architectural Pillars

4,345 Chennai Localities

Covers Greater Chennai Corporation, Tambaram, Avadi, and emerging peripheral industrial and tech corridors including OMR, GST Road, and Sriperumbudur.

Google Places Ground Truth

Live queries to Google Places API (New) pull competitor pins, user ratings, and reviews to confirm site viability before making lease commitments.

Multi-Factor Scoring

Balances estimated commercial rent, footfall traffic, transit connectivity, and competitor density into a normalized 0–100 suitability index.

Hybrid Persistence

Fast local SQLite caching paired with Google Cloud Firestore serverless synchronization for user searches, saved preferences, and favorites.

How the 4-Step Analysis Works

1

Spatial Filtering

Uses the Haversine formula to compute geodesic distances across all 4,345 localities, isolating candidates within a user-selected 1 km to 15 km radius of the anchor point.

2

Weighted Normalization

Applies customizable weights (Rent, Footfall, Transit Access, Competition) summing to 100%, or selects from retail presets tuned for clinics, restaurants, IT services, and auto workshops.

3

Unsupervised K-Means Clustering

Groups localities into 5 distinct economic archetypes using scikit-learn: High Commercial Density, Emerging Suburban, Balanced Urban, Budget Residential, and Premium Enclaves.

4

Ground Truth Verification

Displays ranked candidates on an interactive map alongside competitor establishments, estimated rent bands, and side-by-side trade-off metrics.

Technology Stack & Data Sources

The platform operates on a Python FastAPI backend with mathematical routines in NumPy and scikit-learn. The client utilizes modular CSS and JavaScript for responsive performance, alongside a companion Next.js application in the repository.
