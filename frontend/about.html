About Locus Copilot

Next-generation geospatial intelligence platform empowering entrepreneurs, retail brands, and multi-location operators to discover, evaluate, and rank commercial locations across Chennai with mathematical confidence.

The Challenge

Choosing where to establish or expand a business has historically relied on gut feeling, broker hearsay, or static surveys that lag market reality. Setting up in an oversaturated corridor or an inaccessible transit dead-zone costs businesses millions in sunk capital. Locus Copilot transforms commercial site selection into an algorithmic science—combining granular micro-locality analytics with live Google Places ground truth intelligence.

How It Works (The 4-Step Pipeline)

1

Spatial Geofencing & Proximity Calculation

Applies the Haversine formula to compute geodesic distances across all 4,345 micro-localities, instantly isolating viable candidates within a user-defined 1km to 20km radius.

2

Multi-Factor Weighted Scoring

Applies customizable criteria weights (Rent, Footfall, Transit Proximity, Saturation) or machine learning-optimized presets tailored to your business sector.

3

Unsupervised K-Means Zoning

Automatically clusters localities into 5 distinct economic profiles: High Commercial Density, Emerging Suburban, Balanced Urban, Budget Residential, and Premium Enclaves.

4

Live Ground Truth Verification

For top candidates, real-time establishment pins, verified review ratings, and footfall proxies are fetched directly from Google Maps Platform to confirm site viability before capital is committed.

Restaurant

Fast Casual Outlet

Find high-footfall corridors with manageable rent pressure and dense nearby demand signals from POI activity.

Best for: Cafes, cloud-kitchen hubs, dine-in concepts.
Laptop

Electronics Service Desk

Target productivity and education clusters where users repeatedly need repair, accessory, and upgrade support.

Best for: Repair stores, accessory retail, tech kiosks.

Best for: Phone repair, SIM support, accessories.

Best for: Mechanic shops, quick-service garages.
Stationery

Education Cluster Store

Identify localities near schools and coaching ecosystems where daily utility purchases drive repeat business.

Best for: Books, exam prep, print and supply shops.
