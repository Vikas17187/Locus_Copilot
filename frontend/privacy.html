Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Transparent disclosure of data practices

Information We Collect

When you create an account or interact with Locus Copilot, we process:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, email address, and a salted bcrypt-hashed password.
  • User Preferences: Custom multi-criteria scoring weights (Rent, Footfall, Transit, Competition).
  • Activity Records: Search queries (chosen anchor locality, business category, search radius) and bookmarked favorite locations.
  • Contact Inquiries: Name, email address, subject line, and message content submitted through the contact form.

Third-Party Services

Locus Copilot integrates external platforms necessary to provide map visualization, typography, and live venue verification:

  • Google Maps Platform: Loads vector map tiles, geocoding, and renders interactive map pins. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Places API (New): Performs live queries for competitor density and place ratings in Chennai micro-localities.
  • Google Fonts: Delivers typography assets (IBM Plex Sans and Noto Sans Tamil) via Google's global CDN.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Synchronizes user accounts, saved searches, criteria weights, and favorites across serverless instances.

We do not sell personal information or deploy commercial advertising trackers.

Data Storage & Retention

Account data and user configurations are persisted in Google Cloud Firestore with a local SQLite fallback database during offline or testing runs. You may delete saved favorites or remove your preferences at any time from your account dashboard.

Local Storage Usage

We use browser localStorage solely for operational preferences:

  • theme: Records your chosen interface mode (light or dark) when explicitly toggled.
  • token, email, is_admin: Maintains your authenticated session state across navigation.
