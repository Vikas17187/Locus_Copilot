Analytical Architecture

How Locus Copilot Evaluates Candidate Sites

A deterministic, 4-step spatial multi-criteria decision algorithm that turns raw urban registries into ranked, actionable storefront locations.

STAGE 01

Spatial Boundary Filtering

You select a focal reference landmark and custom radius (1–15 km). The engine runs sub-meter Haversine spherical distance geofencing to immediately eliminate non-viable candidates.

STAGE 02

Multi-Factor Normalization

Extracts point-of-interest density, retail clustering, CMRL metro/MTC bus nodes, and square-foot commercial rental benchmarks, applying min-max z-scaling across all features.

STAGE 03

Vertical Strategy Tuning

Linked weight sliders adjust criteria balance dynamically. A cafe shifts priorities to footfall gravity (45%), while an auto garage weights rental footprint affordability (45%).

STAGE 04

Places Ground-Truth Validation

Top algorithmic candidates are validated in real-time against Google Places API to verify existing competitors, review volume velocity, and storefront saturation before you sign a lease.
