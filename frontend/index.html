How Locus Copilot Ranks Locations

A transparent 4-step decision algorithm that transforms raw urban data into site recommendations.

1

Spatial Boundary Filtering

You select a focal reference location and radius (1–15 km). The engine calculates exact Haversine spherical distances to extract candidate commercial zones.

2

Multi-Criteria Aggregation

Extracts point-of-interest density, retail shop concentrations, metro/bus transit stops, and square-foot commercial rental benchmarks.

3

Vertical Strategy Tuning

Each business type applies distinct criteria weights. For instance, restaurants prioritize foot traffic, while automobile centers prioritize affordable footprint.

4

Places Ground-Truth Validation

Top candidates are verified against Google Places to assess verified competitors, review velocity, and real-time market saturation.

Data Sources, Freshness & Known Limits

Honest disclosures regarding urban data provenance and modeling constraints.

Provenances & Updates

  • Commercial POIs: Overture Maps & OpenStreetMap Chennai registries.
  • Transit Stops: Metropolitan Transport Corporation (MTC) and Chennai Metro Rail (CMRL) network layers.
  • Rental Benchmarks: Locality-level commercial survey estimates compiled for demonstration purposes.
  • Live Competitors: Google Places API (New) cached for 14 days to preserve quota limits.

Operational Constraints

  • Geographic Scope: Currently tuned strictly for the Greater Chennai metropolitan area.
  • Radius Limits: Capped between 1 km and 15 km to ensure computational precision.
  • Decision Support Only: Scores provide algorithmic prioritization; physical site diligence and lease verification are always recommended.
